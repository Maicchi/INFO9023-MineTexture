Welcome to Minetexture

Generate your own unique Minecraft textures !

How to generate textures ?

Make a prompt following this template: Item-name Item-type Keywords
- Item-name: the name of the item
- Item-type: either "item" or "block"
- Keywords: adjectives or details you want to add to your texture.
Some examples of prompts:

"Flower item floral-themed pink nature-magic"

"Quartz block blue vanilla mystical-altar magical_fantasy"

"Pickaxe item purple fantasy-tech magical_fantasy"

Try it yourself:

For better results, also include any of these as keywords:
magical, floral-themed, nature-magic, techno-fantasy, vibrant-colors, arcane-energy, mystical-altar, ornamental, fantasy-tech, vanilla, magical_fantasy
